📦 Amazon Demand Forecasting

How Amazon predicts what you'll buy before you even know it

Demand Prediction & Inventory Management

Problem Description

Amazon operates over 175 fulfillment centers globally and handles millions of products. Predicting demand accurately is crucial to ensure products are in stock when customers want them, while minimizing storage costs and avoiding overstock situations.

Type of Machine Learning

Time Series Forecasting + Regression Models + Deep Learning

Supervised Learning Time Series Analysis

Input Data

  • Historical sales data (past 3-5 years)
  • Seasonal patterns (holidays, Prime Day, Black Friday)
  • Product attributes (category, size, weight, price)
  • Customer behavior data (wish lists, cart additions)
  • External factors (weather, economic indicators)
  • Competitor pricing and promotions

Expected Output

  • Daily/weekly demand forecasts for each SKU
  • Optimal inventory levels across fulfillment centers
  • Anticipatory shipping predictions (sending products before order)
  • Dynamic pricing recommendations
  • Supply chain optimization alerts

Real-World Relevance

Amazon's demand forecasting system uses predictive analytics to anticipate customer purchases, enabling them to implement "anticipatory shipping" - moving products to warehouses near likely buyers before orders are placed. This reduces delivery times from days to hours and has helped Amazon maintain its competitive edge in e-commerce.

Impact: Amazon's machine learning systems have reduced inventory carrying costs by 10-20% while maintaining a 98% in-stock rate for popular items, saving the company billions annually.
