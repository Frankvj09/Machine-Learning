Final Interpretation

The K-Means algorithm successfully identified 3 natural customer segments in the dataset. Total variance was reduced by 66% from Iteration 1 to 3, confirming convergence and meaningful cluster separation.

C1 (Red): Middle-aged customers (≈39 yrs), moderate-high income (~$5,030/mo) — ideal for mid-range to premium products.

C2 (Blue): Young customers (≈24 yrs), lower income (~$1,945/mo) — target with affordable, value-focused offers.

C3 (Green): Senior customers (≈56 yrs), high income (~$7,490/mo) — target with premium, loyalty programs.
