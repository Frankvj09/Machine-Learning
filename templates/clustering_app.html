Dataset Context

This application segments {{ total }} retail customers using K-Means (K=3). Each customer is described by three features: age, annual income, and monthly spending. The goal is to identify natural customer groups to enable targeted marketing strategies.

Preprocessing: All features are standardized using StandardScaler (zero mean, unit variance) before training, ensuring no single feature dominates the distance calculations. The model uses KMeans(n_clusters=3, n_init=10, random_state=42).

Results Interpretation

🔴 Cluster 1 — Young / Low Income

Young customers (~24 yrs) with lower income and spending. Respond well to discounts, entry-level products, and loyalty reward programs.

🔵 Cluster 2 — Middle-Aged / Mid Income

Core customer segment (~40 yrs). Stable income and moderate spending. Target with family products, bundles, and membership programs.

🟢 Cluster 3 — Senior / High Income

High-value customers (~57 yrs) with significant disposable income. Target with premium products, exclusive services, and personalized experiences.

Business Insight: K-Means effectively segmented customers into 3 actionable groups. Cluster 3 customers spend the most despite representing a smaller portion of the base — investing in retention strategies for this segment will yield the highest ROI. Cluster 1 offers growth potential through conversion to higher spending tiers over time.
